## 🔑 Common questions

**Where do I get a Steam Web API key?**
See the [documentation hub](https://docs.helix-origin.club/depressurizer/) for step-by-step
instructions. A key plus your SteamID64 is all the app needs — no Steam client access required.

**My categories did not stick.**
Fully exit the Steam client, apply your changes in Depressurizer, then start Steam again. This is
known flakiness on Valve's side and applies to the desktop client only.
